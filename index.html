High-Impact Practice Fidelity Matrix

4-Quadrant framework evaluating high vs. low fidelity implementation across disciplinary contexts.

AAC&U Aligned
Quadrant I • High Fidelity / High Impact

Transformational Learning (Gold Standard)

Integrates 6+ quality elements (e.g., intensive feedback, public presentation, deep reflection). Produces measurable mastery of TAMU University Learning Outcomes (ULOs).

Quadrant II • High Potential / Low Fidelity

HIP "In Name Only" (Needs Scaffolding)

Designated as a capstone, internship, or research course, but lacks iterative feedback, public showcase, or structured synthesis. High potential requiring structural upgrades.

Quadrant III • High Workload / Low Impact

Transactional Effort (Faculty Burnout Risk)

Heavy grading burden or lengthy assignments without clear connection to real-world application, peer interaction, or self-reflection. High effort without matching impact.

Quadrant IV • Conventional Instruction

Standard Didactic Design

Traditional lecture and exam format. Useful for broad content delivery, but does not fulfill high-impact experiential learning or professional competency goals.
